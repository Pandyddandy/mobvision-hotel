Opening your world…

Your guide to Orbit

Explore a panorama, build the prompt for your next viewpoint, and repair its wrap seam when needed. This guide opens on your first visit; use ? to open it again anytime.

1. Open and explore

Choose or drop a full 360° × 180° equirectangular image, with a 2:1 width-to-height ratio. JPEG, PNG, and WebP are good choices. A regular wide photo will not contain every direction.

Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. With the panorama focused, use the arrow keys to turn, + / − to zoom, and Home to reset. The ↺ button also resets the view. Esc closes this guide first; from the viewer it returns to the image picker.

2. Generate the next viewpoint

  1. Open Next frame. The direction follows your rotation: 0° front, −90° left, +90° right, ±180° back. You can enter it manually; turning again resumes autofill.
  2. Set the distance in meters (starts at 5 m) and add optional scene notes.
  3. Copy the prompt into GPT and attach the current full panorama as Image 1. The viewer builds the prompt; generate the image in GPT.
  4. Use Load generated frame to explore the result, then repeat with that new panorama as your reference.

Each frame keeps the same original front direction. Distance, parallax, and hidden geometry are estimates from a single image; inspect the result for scene drift.

3. Repair the wrap seam

  1. Load the panorama with the broken join, then open Next frame → Repair the wrap seam.
  2. Choose a repair band (starts at 20%; try a wider band for large misaligned structures). Download a fresh repair reference. The viewer moves the join to the center and covers it with a magenta strip.
  3. Attach that downloaded reference to GPT and copy its matching repair prompt. Ask GPT to replace the magenta strip with continuous scene content. The optional edit mask is for tools with a dedicated mask input.
  4. Import GPT’s result with Preview GPT repair. Keep the exact reference dimensions and framing. Compare the before/after join: changed pixels alone do not prove the repair worked.
  5. Apply candidate only if the geometry connects. The viewer blends just the band, preserves the surrounding image, and restores the original front. Save the fixed panorama and use it for your next GPT reference.

Keep this page open during seam repair; its reference and settings stay in memory. If GPT leaves the break or placeholder intact, regenerate from the fresh reference or try a wider band.
